Parakeet Ultra

Parakeet Ultra by moondream is derived from NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3.
The app uses the Core ML conversion published by FluidInference, with an int8
encoder and the Ultra decoder and joint model. The app does not modify these
published weights.
