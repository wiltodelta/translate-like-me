# Product direction

Translate Like Me is one everyday writing app for dictating, improving, and
translating text in the user's own style, wherever they are already writing.
The shared outcome is a finished message that sounds like its author.

The intended flow starts with speech or typed text, continues through refinement,
and adds translation when needed. Each action remains useful on its own.
Personal style connects refinement and translation; dictation supplies the
original words.

## Product priorities

- One coherent writing experience across dictation, Rewrite, and translation.
- A small native interface that stays out of the writing task.
- Installed and immediately useful is a product requirement: automatically
  recommend a working configuration for the person and their Mac, with useful
  defaults and only unavoidable permissions and preparation before first use.
- Optional personalization that improves the result without making an extensive
  writing guide or model tuning a prerequisite.
- The user's meaning and voice remain central when improving or translating text.

These priorities describe the intended product experience. They do not imply
that every setup step is already automatic. Today, permissions and model
preparation are required; CLI engines need installation and sign-in, personal
styles are supplied by the user, and fresh profiles receive a neutral Rewrite
preset while existing custom presets remain unchanged.
Dictation, Rewrite, and translation currently have separate shortcuts.

The [README](../README.md) presents this experience to new users. The
[user guide](user-guide.md) documents the current setup and behavior.
