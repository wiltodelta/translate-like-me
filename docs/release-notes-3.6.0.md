Translate Like Me 3.6.0

- Dictation now has its own tab in Settings, with the hold key, speech model and preparation status in one place.
- Choose Whisper Small, Whisper Large v3 Turbo or Whisper Large v3. Turbo remains the recommended default. Each model has a short description and its download size.
- Models download automatically while dictation is enabled. Your choice is saved, and downloaded models stay cached for later use.
